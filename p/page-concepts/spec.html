How Zana builds a gym page

The idea

Think of toy bricks. Zana keeps a box of blocks, a paint set called tokens and motion switches called effects. A page is a recipe: which blocks, in what order, painted how. Gym data sits apart, so any recipe fits any gym.

Picture itMarisol opens a pilates studio in Ocean Park. Tuesday night she uploads her logo and six photos, picks terracotta and the Calm style. Her schedule and prices are already in Zana, so the page builds itself. Wednesday the link is in her social bio.

Blocks

Seventeen, plus the Book button: menu bar, five heroes (film, slideshow, split, framed photo, three tiles), offer strip, statement, class cards, two schedules, price cards, coaches, reviews, first visit, booking bar, footer.

Zana writes the headline and facts from the data. A block with nothing to show hides itself. No coach photo? Initials.

Tokens

  • Color. One brand color in, a readable set out. A guard fixes weak contrast, so pale yellow still works.
  • Light or dark.
  • Type. Four heading voices. The headline stays 2.5 to 3 times the body. Body is 17 to 18px.
  • Spacing. Compact, comfortable, airy.
  • Corners. Square, soft, round.
  • Photos. Original, black and white, brand tinted.

Owner settings

Owners see a recipe as a style. It sets every default. The owner changes a short list, each within limits, each with a reset. Try the mock.

  • At launch: style, brand color, logo, cover photo, spacing, headline size, sections on or off, four effect switches, photo hero.
  • Later: slideshow and video hero, four more effect switches, section order.
  • Stays with the style: light or dark, corners, typeface, photo look.

My pushback: section order can wait. Styles already order a page well, and order is the knob most likely to bury the schedule.

Build plan

One developer with Claude Code. Opus at high effort builds, Fable reviews. Rough numbers.

1. FoundationAbout 3.5 weeks

Tokens. Twelve blocks: menu bar, three still heroes, offer strip, statement, class cards, both schedules, price cards, coaches, footer. Book button. Four effects. The launch settings with live preview. Photo upload. English and Spanish.

2. Rich mediaAbout 2.5 weeks

Video upload and compression. Slideshow and framed heroes. Five more effects. Booking bar, reviews, first visit. Brand tinted photos. The later settings.

Everything up front: about 6 weeks before any gym has a page.

My pick: foundation first. All four looks ship in phase 1 with still photos and the core blocks. Video is the slow, risky part: heavy files, picky phones.

Example: a boxing gym that signs up in week four gets its page that day. Its video hero arrives later, nothing to redo.

How we know it works

  • 70% of new gyms publish a page in their first week.
  • Setup takes under 10 minutes.
  • 5% of page visits end in a booking or a purchase.
  • The page shows in under 2.5 seconds on a phone.

One decision for you

Calm uses a serif, Mono a tall condensed face. Zana's rule is one self-hosted typeface. Allow three on public gym pages, or ship those two in Geist with less character.
